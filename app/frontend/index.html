How Smart Pack helps

The right packaging helps protect food from air, moisture, and damage while it is stored and shipped.

Different foods need different kinds of protection. Fresh produce may need to breathe, while dry snacks need to stay away from air and dampness.

Enter a product and its storage and travel details to see suitable packaging, pack layers, a protective gas mix when useful, freshness and cost estimates, greener options, and a code for the saved plan.

What goes into a suggestion?

Suggestions take into account the food, storage temperature, shipping method, batch size, budget, and material details in the catalogue.

The results include the best matches, why they may suit your product, expected freshness, cost, storage tips, and possible trade-offs. Estimates can help you plan; confirm important packaging choices with a real pack test.
