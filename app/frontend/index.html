Smart Pack AI System Architecture

Packaging holds food quality, safety, and shelf life through storage, transport, and distribution. Choosing inappropriate film stock permits moisture ingress, oxygen breakdown, or gas imbalances — triggering rapid spoilage, texture loss, and nutrient degradation.

Fresh agricultural commodities continue respiring after harvest. Consequently, Oxygen Transmission Rate (OTR), Water Vapor Transmission Rate (WVTR), and Carbon Dioxide Transmission Rate (CO₂TR) must be specified quantitatively according to crop physical metrics rather than arbitrary selection.

Smart Pack AI analyzes food physical parameters (moisture, fat, pH, respiration class) alongside distribution logistics (temperature, RH, transit vibration, lot size) to recommend ideal mill materials, gauge thickness, multilayer structures, MAP gas formulations, shelf-life estimates, cost projections, and QR lot traceability.

Methodology & Mathematical Engine

Data sources: USDA FoodData Central · FAO Post-Harvest Handbooks · ASTM F1383 · ISO 15105-1 · Robertson (2013) Food Packaging Principles · Farber & Dodds MAP Formulations.

Hybrid AI Engine: A scikit-learn TF-IDF model ranks materials locally using product details, material descriptions, barrier similarity, and packaging constraints. When a Gemini API key is configured, Gemini explains the top-ranked option in everyday language, including why it fits, a trade-off, and any assumptions. Local recommendations still work if Gemini is unavailable.
